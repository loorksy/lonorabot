## Finding 1 (medium): the owner's correction is silently overwritten by Lonora's next observation (8.4)

- `correct_line` records the owner's revision, but nothing stops a later agent patch or remove of that line, and Lonora's context does not mark which lines are the owner's word.
- Evidence: probe on a fresh database. Lonora observes "- Reads at 07:30", the owner corrects it to "- Reads at 08:00", and a later Lonora routine run patches it back:
  - `AFTER_CORRECTION [{'text': '- Reads at 08:00', 'origin': 'you_said', 'author': 'owner'…}]`
  - `LONORA_CONTEXT_MARKS_CORRECTION False`
  - `PATCH True None True`
  - `AFTER_LONORA [{'text': '- Reads at 07:30', 'origin': 'observed', 'author': 'agent', 'author_agent_name': 'Lonora'…}]`

  The owner is not told.
- Fix: in `lonora_tools/memory.py` write(), refuse an agent patch or remove of a USER.md line whose latest origin is the owner's word (use `profile_lines`) unless the run carries `owner_message_id`. Show Lonora which lines are the owner's word, and add a test.

## Finding 2 (low): a line's origin is computed wrongly in two cases

- File: `packages/db/src/lonora_db/owner_knowledge.py` (`profile_lines`), where `said = revision.author == "owner" or …`.
- An owner revert is recorded with `author="owner"`, so restoring an observed line relabels it "You said".
- A legacy line with no matching revision shows as "Observed".
- Fix: for a revert, take the origin from the reverted revision. Give unmatched lines their own origin.
