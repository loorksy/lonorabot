## Finding 1 (medium): a framed peer message breaks the voice context byte budget

- File: `packages/voice/src/lonora_voice/context.py:218-221`.
- Evidence: the peer text is bounded to `available - 600` bytes and only then framed. `peer_content` escapes `<`, `>` and `&` into 4- or 5-byte entities and adds a frame of about 513 bytes, so the framed entry is not bounded by `available`. With `bounded_text('<'*3000, 2400)`, the framed entry serializes to 10,056 bytes. Probe in the existing voice fixture (`tests/voice/test_context.py` helpers), on a fresh database: three peer messages of `"if (a < b && c > d) " * 200` in the thread produced `SIZE 8530 MAX 7500 entries 1`, failing the invariant `size <= MAX_CONTEXT_BYTES` that `test_unicode_and_escaped_context_stay_bounded_without_mutating_memory` pins. The comment "bounded before framing, so the frame always closes" holds, but the budget does not. Before this PR, the entry was `bounded_text(content, available)` and always fit.
- Fix: bound the framed result rather than the raw text. For example, shrink the inner budget until `len(_json(_message(role, peer_content(name, bounded_text(text, inner)))).encode()) - 2 <= available`, by halving or by subtracting the measured overflow, with a floor at which the entry is skipped. Alternatively, bound the escaped body directly. Add a voice test with a peer message full of `<` and `&` that asserts `size <= MAX_CONTEXT_BYTES`.
