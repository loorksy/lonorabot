## Findings

### M1. A skill still says a fixed-market agent trades only its bound market (0.4)
- `packages/agent-runtime/resources/skills/market-analysis/SKILL.md:17-19`: "A legacy fixed-market agent uses its exact bound market for charts, watches, decisions and trades".
- This contradicts `lonora.md:224` ("its trades do not") and `BINDING_POLICY` ("Your orders are not bound to this pair") at this commit. Confirmed by reading all three.
- Fix: change it to "for charts, watches and decisions; its orders are not bound by the server", then regenerate the manifest.

### M2. The assigned pair stops reading as an instruction before 14.3.18.4 writes it into the method (3.1.2, 14.1.1)
- 3.1.2 says the instruments Lonora assigns "are instructions in the agent's method"; only the door stops enforcing them. The migration that writes the bound account and symbol into the method (14.3.18.4) is not in this PR; the PR defers it to W-AUTH. Until then, the binding text is the only carrier of the assignment, and the PR words it as a permission to trade anywhere:
  - `BINDING_POLICY` (`agent_binding.py:12-23`): "Your orders are not bound to this pair";
  - `lonora.md:224`: "its trades do not [stay on its focus pair]";
  - `FOCUS_PAIR_NOTE` (`market_binding.py:250-253`): "Reading and trading are open across every owned account and symbol".
- A gold agent in Full therefore has no instruction left to trade gold.
- The door change itself is correct. The subagent's probe showed that an agent bound to A/EURUSD in Full got `confirmed` on B/XAUUSD, as 6.9 intends.
- Fix: keep the door change, and word the text as the agent's assignment, for example: "This pair is your assignment from Lonora or the owner: trade it unless told otherwise. The server does not refuse other symbols." Apply this to all three texts and regenerate the manifest.

### L1. `task_wait(kind="broker_recovery")` still applies the market binding
- `packages/db/src/lonora_db/task_wait.py:42-50` still calls `assert_market_binding(...)`.
- A bound agent may now trade account B, but cannot wait for B's broker recovery (`agent_trading_account_mismatch`). Confirmed by reading the code; not run.
- Fix: drop the assertion; ownership of the source is already checked at `:46`.

### L2. The new "not bound" assertions accept any failure (14.1.3)
- `tests/check_agent_binding.py:134-148` (`not_bound`), its child and peer branches (`:326`, `:387`), and `tests/test_proactive_wakes.py` (~836) accept any `LonoraError` except the two binding codes. A lease or validation failure would also pass.
- No test proves that a bound agent in Full actually sends on another market.
- Fix: assert the exact outcome (`trade_approval_required` or an `approval_id` in Ask), and add a Full case asserting `status == "confirmed"` on another account and symbol.

- The `assert_market_binding` docstring (`agent_binding.py:70`) says "A fixed-market agent's mutations stay on its pair". Orders are no longer bound.
- Fix: update both.
