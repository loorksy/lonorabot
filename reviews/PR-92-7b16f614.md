## Findings

### M1. An owner's answer to a pending question resumes the Run on the frozen head (5.4, 11.8, 0.4)
- `packages/agent-runtime/src/lonora_agent/tool_loop.py:374-410` rebuilds the head only after `resume_after_attention` and after `consume_wake`.
- When the owner replies to a `waiting_input` Run, `packages/db/src/lonora_db/messages.py:536-552` pops `pending_input_request` and requeues the Run. Neither branch fires, and `refresh_after_steering` refreshes only skills.
- `request_owner_attention` (`loop_outcomes.py:121-128`) says "the owner's reply continues it", so the documented way to resume held work reads the stale policy, brief and memory.
- The PR's new docs are untrue for this path: `docs/agent-operating-policy.md` (+126-129) and `docs/context-engine.md` (+140-146) say the owner resuming held work rebuilds the head.
- Reproduced by the subagent's probe `/home/user/wt/probes/pr92/probe_owner_answer.py`: the owner answers through `send_message`, and the woken turn still had the obsolete policy and not the new brief. Independently reproduced on #108 (a ticket opened while waiting was absent after the answer). Control flow confirmed by me.
- Fix:
  - In `messages.py`, where `pending_input_request` is popped, set `state["owner_answered_input"] = {...}`.
  - In `tool_loop.py`, pop it before the `question` check; `if stale_head:` call `refresh_context_head(...)`, set `stale_head = False` and `save()`. Alternatively, before `consume_wake`: `if stale_head and await owner_steering_pending(context): refresh`.
  - Add an `owner_answer` mode to `tests/check_task_wake_context.py`.

### L1. A rebuilt head drops the fresh `<context_omitted>` guidance notice
- `loop_restore.py:98-104` keeps only `data[:1]` of the fresh non-system messages.
- `govern_context` places its guidance notice right after the context data when it sheds optional `runtime_context` records. The rebuilt head can therefore lack those listings without the model being told.
- Reproduced at unit level by the subagent (`probe_head.py`: `guidance omission notice kept: False`).
- Fix: keep the leading context data together with a directly following `<context_omitted>` notice.

### L2. Docstring untrue at this commit (0.4)
- `loop_restore.py:86` says "only `build_context` writes them [system messages] into a checkpoint". `refresh_routing_context` also rewrites the routing-frame system message on every turn. Behaviour is still correct.
- Fix: "system messages in a checkpoint are head-only (`build_context` and the per-turn routing frame)".
