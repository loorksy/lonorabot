## Finding 1 (high): "nothing else needs you" is said while an unknown-outcome operation still waits (11.1, 6.8)

- File: `packages/trading/src/lonora_trading/morning_brief.py:209-219,274`. `unsettled` counts `ambiguous` journal rows only among rows with `updated_at > since`, where `since` is the last brief.
- Evidence: an operation that went ambiguous before yesterday's brief and is still unsettled is not counted today. The brief then sets `nothing_else_needs_you=True`, and the plain brief prints «لا شيء آخر يحتاجك». Probe on PostgreSQL printed `day1 unsettled 1 False` then `day2 unsettled 0 True`, with the row still `ambiguous`.
- Fix: count unsettled operations with their own query, independent of `since`: `status == "ambiguous"` for the owner. Keep `since` only for the per-agent work list. Add the day-2 case to `tests/test_morning_brief.py`.

## Finding 2 (medium): `nothing_else_needs_you` ignores other things that wait for the owner (11.1)

- File: `morning_brief.py:252-274`. Only pending Trade Approvals, ambiguous rows and disconnected accounts are counted.
- Ignored: pending owner cards (`ln_owner_actions`, e.g. `connect_credential`, the Secret card), runs in `waiting_input` (an agent asking the owner a question), and open attention items.
- Evidence: probe with a pending `connect_credential` card printed `owner card pending; nothing_else_needs_you = True`.
- Fix: also require no live pending owner card, no `waiting_input` run of the owner's agents, and no open attention item. Put their counts in the facts, and test each.

## Finding 3 (medium): the learned brief time is not wired, but the doc says it is (0.4)

- The only caller, `packages/gateway/src/lonora_gateway/service.py:259`, calls `brief_tick(self.db, service.owner_id)` with no `first_opens` and no `device_zone`. No module outside this PR records app opens or a device zone (repo grep).
- Result: in production the brief is always 07:00 in `reach_me.timezone`, else UTC.
- The doc claims more: `docs/morning-brief.md:9-13` describes the median-of-first-opens rule, the device-zone fallback "from the app-open record" and the one-hour move as current behavior. The docstrings near `owner_zone` and `brief_tick` say the same.
- Fix: make the doc and docstrings say what is true now (07:00 in `reach_me.timezone`, else UTC; learning starts when the app-open record lands). Do not claim the "time is learned" clause here. Either drop the unfed parameters until that record exists, or mark them as not yet fed.
