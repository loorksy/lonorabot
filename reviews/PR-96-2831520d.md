## Findings

### M1. "Unknown" ends broker reconciliation while the outcome is still unknown (6.3, 10.3)
- `apps/api/src/lonora_api/routes/owner_actions.py:406-425` sends every answer, `unknown` included, to `attest_operation`, which reaches `resolve_operation` (`packages/db/src/lonora_db/trading_journal.py:186-190`), which sets `status="resolved"`.
- `resolved` is in `TERMINAL_STATUSES` and not in `UNRESOLVED_STATUSES` (`packages/contracts/src/lonora_contracts/trading.py:141-143`). As a result:
  - the reconciler skips the row (`packages/trading/src/lonora_trading/broker_recovery.py:83`);
  - `persist_result` refuses later broker evidence (`trading_journal.py:101`).
- 6.3 says an operation whose outcome is unknown "is reconciled with the broker". After the Unknown answer it is not, and its hold is released ("Unknown: release the hold", `packages/core/src/owner-actions.ts:91`; Arabic «غير معروفة — ارفع التعليق» at `:372`).
- A broker confirmation that arrives after any answer is dropped, so no Execution Receipt is posted for it (10.3, 11.2).
- The behaviour predates this PR. This PR, however, is the change that declares the card to be 10.3's Settle card with three answers, so it carries the semantics.
- Reproduced by the subagent's probe (`status after Unknown: resolved | reconciler considers it: False`; later broker evidence `applied: False`) and confirmed by reading the four sites above.
- Fix:
  - For `outcome == "unknown"`, record the owner's answer on the journal row (`details.owner_answers[]`, source owner, `broker_confirmed: false`, plus an event) and leave `status="ambiguous"`, so reconciliation continues and `persist_result` can still confirm it and post the receipt.
  - Settle only the card (`confirmed`, result `{outcome: "unknown"}`).
  - Keep the hold and drop "release the hold" from the label and hint in both languages.
  - Test: answer Unknown, then a broker observation confirms; assert the row is `confirmed`, one receipt exists, and no resend happened.
