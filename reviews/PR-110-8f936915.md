## Finding 1 (medium): two Mandate cards for one agent give two items and two pushes; tapping one leaves the other open

- Files:
  - `packages/core/src/lonora_core/attention.py:36-41`: `CARD_ATTENTION["trading_mode"]` makes an item per pending card.
  - `packages/gateway/src/lonora_gateway/attention.py:178-184`: the item scope is `owner_action:<card id>`.
  - `packages/db/src/lonora_db/attention.py:130-137`: the item stays current while its own card is pending.
  - `apps/api/src/lonora_api/routes/owner_actions.py:207-226`: `decide_trading_mode` settles only the tapped card.
- Evidence: cards are deduplicated per proposer. Agent X proposing its own card and Lonora proposing one for X therefore give two pending `trading_mode:X` cards. Probe on a fresh database (`owner_action_propose` from X, then from Lonora with `agent_id=X`, then attention ticks):
  ```
  items for X: [('ready', True), ('ready', False)]
  after tapping X's own card Full: [('own_card', 'suppressed'), ('lonora_card', 'ready')]
  card agent by Lonora pending / card agent by X confirmed
  ```
  The owner receives two "needs your attention" pushes for one decision (11.4: one push per proposed card for one decision). After the tap, an action-required item stays ready for a decision already made (11.7).
- Fix, either of these:
  - (a) In `decide_trading_mode`, after `set_trading_mode`, supersede every other pending `trading_mode` card with the same subject: `supersede_subject(session, owner.id, "trading_mode", row.subject, "mandate_decided", keep=row.id)`. `withdraw_ended` then clears the sibling's item. Also make a second proposal for the same subject return the existing pending card, whoever proposed it. Card deduplication is #73's code, so coordinate with W-AUTH.
  - (b) Scope the item per subject (`mandate:<agent>`), and end it once any `trading_mode` card for that subject is decided.
  - Either way, add the two-proposer case to `tests/check_attention_items.py` (`mandate_card`).
