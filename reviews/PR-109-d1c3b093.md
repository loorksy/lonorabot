## New finding

### L1. On MetaApi, an absent symbol is never recognised as absent
- `metaapi.instrument()` calls `diagnose(error, "instrument")`.
- In `provider_error` (`metaapi.py:52-65`), a 404 at a stage other than `symbol` or `candles` becomes `broker_account_not_found`, so `symbol_not_found` is never produced for this read. `broker_instrument_unavailable` comes only from the simulation broker.
- So a symbol MetaApi truly does not list is retried every 10 minutes forever. It stays honestly under `currencies_unknown`, so the cost is only wasted calls.
- Fix: in `provider_error`, treat stage `instrument` like `symbol` for a 404, or add `broker_account_not_found` from the instrument stage to `INSTRUMENT_ABSENT` only after confirming the account itself is reachable. Add a MetaApi adapter unit case.
