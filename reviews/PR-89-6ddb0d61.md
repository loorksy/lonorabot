## Finding 1 (medium): deleting a skill erases all its versions, so "revert to any version" fails (5.5.5)

- Files:
  - `packages/db/src/lonora_db/schema.py:709-711`: `ln_skill_versions.skill_id` is `ForeignKey("ln_skills.id", ondelete="CASCADE")`.
  - The owner's `DELETE /skills/{id}` (`apps/api/src/lonora_api/routes/agent_skills.py:116-119`) deletes the row.
  - The agent's `skill_package_control action=remove` (`packages/agent-runtime/src/lonora_agent/skills/lifecycle.py:518`) deletes the row too.
- Evidence: probe on a fresh database, `skill_save` twice then the owner's DELETE, printed `versions before delete 2 after 0` and `history after delete: skill_not_found`. A delete is a write to a skill, but it creates no version and destroys every earlier one. The PR's letter-check marks "Every write to a … skill creates a version" as done.
- Fix: make delete a versioned write. Either soft-delete (a deleted state recorded as the next `SkillVersionRow`, with history kept), or let versions outlive the skill row. Let `skill_restore` and the owner's restore bring a deleted skill back. Add a test: save, delete, restore version 1.
