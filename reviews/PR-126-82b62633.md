### M1. The finished/cancel guards of the new pass rule are not pinned (6.9, 14.1.3)
- The new pass rule is in `packages/trading/src/lonora_trading/execution.py:134-141`. Two of its guards survive mutation under the PR's tests:
  - `and state.status not in {"cancelled","completed","failed"}` (`:138`);
  - `and state.control_requested in {None, "pause"}` (`:137`).
- `tests/test_run_integrity.py` reaches only `control_requested="cancel"` with the status still running, which `:142` refuses anyway. It never reaches:
  - status cancelled, completed or failed with a leftover pause;
  - status paused with a cancel or complete request.
- The PR says these checks are pinned.
- Reproduced by the subagent's mutation runs (`run.sh pr126 python -m pytest tests/test_run_integrity.py` passes with either guard removed).
- Fix: add a test that writes `TicketRow.state["task"]` for each case below and asserts `require_task_authority(..., reducing=True)` raises `task_execution_stopped`:
  - (cancelled, pause), (completed, pause), (failed, pause);
  - (paused, cancel), (paused, complete);
  - (cancelled, None).
