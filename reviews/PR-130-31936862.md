## Finding 1 (medium): the report is posted at the weekly close, not on the first morning brief (5.5.6)

- Files:
  - `packages/trading/src/lonora_trading/weekly_report.py:378-429`: `weekly_tick` wakes every agent once `latest_close(now)` has passed (Friday 17:00 New York).
  - It runs as its own gateway step every 30 s (`packages/gateway/src/lonora_gateway/service.py:66,263-266`).
- The PR's test does this at CLOSE + 1 min (`tests/test_weekly_report.py:543`). The mandate says "on the first morning brief after the weekly market close", and the map's Q12 says it is fired by the brief dispatcher.
- For a Riyadh owner, reports post at about 01:00 Saturday, roughly six hours before the brief. The 60-minute brief hold after the close (`REPORT_WAIT`, `morning_brief.py:65-67,175-190`) is not in the mandate.
- Fix: start the report wakes from `brief_tick` when the brief is due and `latest_close` is newer than `record.weekly["close"]`. Hold the brief with a wait measured from those wakes, then send it with the reports, and drop the separate `weekly_reports` step.

## Finding 2 (medium): Lonora can miss the reports in "that brief"

- Cause: two independent loops, and the wait is counted from the close.
- Probe on a fresh database, Riyadh zone, brief due at 07:01 local:
  - Brief step before the weekly step (e.g. after a restart): `brief: 2026-11-14`, then `weekly: 2026-11-13`, and the first brief had `weekly_reports: []`. The reports reach only the next day's brief.
  - Weekly step more than 60 min after the close: the brief went at once with every entry `not_ready`, and the week was marked summarized (`morning_brief.py:179-180,664-666`). Reports written afterwards are never summarized.
- Fix: finding 1's fix removes both cases. Short of it, store `woken_at` and count the wait from it, and make `brief_tick` hold while `record.weekly` is older than `latest_close(now)`.

## Finding 3 (medium): agents' own report text is presented to Lonora as journal and broker records (5.3.3, 6.10, 0.4)

- `weekly_reports()` (`morning_brief.py:193-217`) puts up to 1,500 characters of each agent's message into `<brief_facts>`.
- The wake text still says "The facts are records of the journal, the broker and the approval cards" and "never state a profit or an execution the facts do not show" (`morning_brief.py:374-377`).
- So a profit an agent claims in its report counts as a fact, and Lonora may repeat it as broker-confirmed.
- `docs/morning-brief.md:33-34` says the facts come "never from an agent's sentence", which is false at this commit.
- Authority does not widen: the triggers are not in `OWNER_TRIGGERS`.
- Fix: carry the reports in their own block (e.g. `<agent_reports>`) described as each agent's own words, as peer data and not broker evidence. State profits and executions only from journal and broker facts, and correct the doc.

## Finding 4 (low): a close up to 7 days old is woken mid-week

- `latest_close` looks back up to 15 days, and `weekly_tick` wakes it whenever `record.weekly` is None. The PR's own test shows `weekly_tick(now=CLOSE - 1min) == "2026-11-06"` (`test_weekly_report.py:538`).
- A deploy on Thursday makes every agent report on last week, and that is not "the first brief after the close".
- Fix: skip a close when a brief was already sent after it (`record.last_sent_at > close`). Finding 1's fix covers this.
