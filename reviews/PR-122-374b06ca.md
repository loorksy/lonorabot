## Verified findings

### M1 (medium, 7.2.6): rotated and deleted values are scanned only until the supervisor restarts
- `given` lives in memory only. After a supervisor restart (every `lonora update`, container restart or host reboot), an old background log on the persistent disk was read in clear: `old log after restart: 'old=s3cr3t-Value-0123456789abcdefXYZ\n'`.
- Fix: keep rotated and deleted values on the Gateway side. On rotate or delete, the secret store keeps the previous value encrypted (for example a `previous` ciphertext per secret, or a small `ln_secret_history` table, pruned after a bounded time). Then `scanned_values()` includes those. This is durable and needs no supervisor memory, and it keeps secrets off the workspace disk.

### M2 (medium, 7.2.6 design): every supervisor response carries every secret value given since boot
- `supervisor.py:553,561,609,615,642,650` add `"guard": self.guard()` to `/v1/run`, `/v1/processes`, `/v1/files/read` and `/v1/files/write`.
- Measured: 41 pairs made a `/v1/processes` response 642,824 bytes. The bound is 1000 pairs × up to 16,000 characters per value.
- Every caller must pop `guard` before using a response. The owner's "What is running" view (7.1.5) and any future caller or debug print of `/v1/processes` would leak every value. Responses also grow without a size bound.
- Fix: a separate `GET /v1/guard` used only by the client's scan builder (or, with M1's Gateway-side history, names only and no values), and never mix values into data responses. Bound the total size.

### L1 (low, 7.2.6): the new partial-match minimum shows up to a third of a long secret at a cut
- The minimum is `max(PARTIAL=12, len(form)//3)`. At a reader-cancel cut or EOF, a prefix just under it is shown: a 31-character value showed `'sk-ant-api0'` (11 characters), and a 64-character value showed `'ghp_Z9y8X7w6V5u4T3s2'` (20 characters). The timeout path is unaffected (margin trim).
- Fix: keep the minimum near 8, but require the partial to start at a token boundary (start of line, or after whitespace, `=`, `:`, a quote). That still avoids the false positives.

- The socket transport of the guard values: the control directory is root:10001 0750 and nothing logs bodies. The risk is the design (M2), not the transport.
