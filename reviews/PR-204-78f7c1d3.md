## Finding 1 (medium, 3.3.4 in the owner's brief): the journal read keeps the oldest 5000 entries, so a recent agent's ticket reads as the owner's

`packages/trading/src/lonora_trading/morning_brief.py`, `journaled_openers`:

- It changed `.order_by(TradingJournalRow.updated_at.desc()).limit(5000)` to `.order_by(TradingJournalRow.updated_at).limit(5000)`.
- With more than 5000 entry orders on the open symbols, the newest are now cut. Those are the ones most likely to have opened today's open tickets, and those tickets then read "owner".
- Main keeps the newest 5000.

Probe, run with the test file's own fixture and `seed`:

- 5000 owner entry orders on XAUUSD, journaled 30 days earlier, plus the seeded gold-agent entry `op-open` from 3 hours before.
- Result: `open_positions[*].opened_by` is `['owner', 'owner']`. The expected value is `[gold, 'owner']`, as `test_lonora_is_woken_once_per_local_date_with_the_facts` asserts without the extra rows.

Fix:

- Keep the newest window and apply "earliest wins" inside it: `.order_by(TradingJournalRow.updated_at.desc()).limit(5000)`, then `return ticket_openers(list(rows)[::-1])`.
- Add the probe as a test.
- Optional, for exactness: the row has no `created_at`, and `updated_at` moves when an ambiguous entry is later resolved. Ordering by `submitted_at`, then `updated_at`, is closer to "earliest journaled".
