## Findings

### M1. The frame text is not true for `own_results` (0.4, 5.3.3)
The text is in `packages/agent-runtime/src/lonora_agent/context/picture.py:326-330`. Three things are wrong:
- **(a) Unpriced closes.** The text says "net realized P&L from broker deals (null when the deals do not give it)". A label with both priced and unpriced closes returns a partial net plus an `unpriced` count, which the frame never explains. Probe: `{'count': 2, 'net_realized_pnl': '5', 'unpriced': 1}`.
- **(b) Silent truncation.** `statistics_block` returns at most 20 rows (`MAXIMUM_ROWS`) with no not-shown count. The frame's "the not_shown counts give the rest" is therefore untrue here. Probe: 28 groups, 20 returned, nothing said.
- **(c) No time window.** The figures are all-time over every account, starting 30 days before each connection was made (`FIRST_LOOKBACK`). The frame states none of this.

All three were reproduced by the subagent's probe; I confirmed the frame text by reading it.

Fix:
- Reword the frame: "Your results, since the ledger began (broker deal history from 30 days before each connection), on every account: your closed trades per opening label and account currency, with count and net realized P&L from broker deals; closes whose P&L the deals do not give are counted in unpriced and add nothing, and a row with none priced has net null; the 20 most used rows."
- Better: have `statistics_block` return a not-shown count (W-LEDGER).

### L1. Test coverage
`check_account_picture` does not cover a partially unpriced row, a second currency, or the row bound.
Fix: add a mixed row asserting `unpriced == 1`, and an EUR trade asserting its own row.
